Learning Experience Studio

Building capability that improves organizational performance.

The Learning Experience Studio helps organizations design practical learning experiences that build capability, support organizational change, and enable people to perform with greater confidence and consistency.

The Challenge

  • Training that is disconnected from business objectives.
  • Employees completing courses without changing workplace behaviors.
  • Managers lacking the tools to reinforce learning after training.
  • Learning programs that struggle to keep pace with organizational change.
  • AI and digital transformation initiatives that outpace workforce capability.
  • Limited measurement beyond participation and completion rates.

Effective transformation begins with improving how the organization works.

What is the Learning Experience Studio?

The Learning Experience Studio is Apprendii's consulting approach for designing learning strategies and experiences that improve organizational capability and support lasting performance.

Rather than focusing solely on course development, the Studio aligns learning with business goals, workforce needs, leadership expectations, and organizational priorities to create meaningful and measurable learning experiences. Every solution is designed with the learner, the manager, and the organization in mind.

Who It’s For

  • Strengthen workforce capability.
  • Support organizational transformation.
  • Improve onboarding and employee development.
  • Increase the effectiveness of leadership and manager development.
  • Modernize learning strategies and programs.
  • Align learning investments with business outcomes.
How We Work

A focused path from understanding to implementation.

01

Discovery

Understanding business priorities, workforce needs, organizational challenges, and existing learning programs.

02

Learning Assessment

Evaluating current learning strategies, learner experiences, performance expectations, and opportunities for improvement.

03

Experience Design

Designing practical learning experiences, learning journeys, and supporting resources that build capability and improve performance.

04

Continuous Improvement

Evaluating outcomes, gathering feedback, and refining learning experiences to ensure they continue supporting organizational goals.

Typical Outcomes
Stronger workforce capability.
Faster employee readiness.
Improved manager support for learning.
Greater alignment between learning and business strategy.
More engaging and practical learning experiences.
Sustainable performance improvement.

Why Apprendii

The Learning Experience Studio draws on more than twenty-five years of experience in organizational learning, instructional design, leadership development, technology enablement, and organizational transformation. Rather than creating learning for its own sake, the Studio helps organizations build capability that improves performance, supports change, and enables long-term success.
