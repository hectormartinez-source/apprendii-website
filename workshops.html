Workshops

Building human capability for AI-enabled work.

Practical learning and working experiences that help leaders, managers, employees, and teams make better decisions and improve how work gets done.

Five ways to get started

Start with the challenge that matters most.

AI creates new possibilities, but making them useful depends on people, judgment, leadership, and the design of work. These five experiences address different needs across an organization. Each can stand alone or complement a broader effort.

01

Executive leaders

Leading AI-Enabled Change

AI opportunities can generate activity without a clear connection to what an organization needs to improve. In this facilitated working session, leaders bring a real challenge to the table, examine the evidence and assumptions behind it, and consider the people, workflows, and organizational conditions affecting progress. The conversation is designed to clarify options, trade-offs, and practical decisions that could move the work forward.

02

Managers and supervisors

Helping Your Team Succeed with AI

Managers are being asked to guide teams through AI adoption, even when employees bring different levels of confidence, experience, and concern. Drawing on Apprendii’s Behavioral Orchestration Framework and AI Orientation Model, this experience explores how managers can set expectations, coach different team members, and support responsible human-AI collaboration. Participants consider practical ways to strengthen team habits, work quality, and everyday application.

03

Individual contributors

Judging AI Outputs

AI-generated work can sound convincing while missing context, evidence, or the point of the task. Participants examine realistic AI outputs, compare judgments about what to accept, verify, revise, or reject, and discuss examples where their own intervention made AI-assisted work better. Through these applied activities, the experience strengthens discernment, critical evaluation, and personal accountability rather than treating a polished response as a finished answer.

04

Employees

Getting Better Results with AI

Employees often have access to AI tools but need more practice directing them toward useful work. In this hands-on experience, participants see an AI-generated result improved through clearer context, examples, and iterative feedback, then apply those approaches to suitable tasks of their own. The focus is on building practical confidence in shaping, reviewing, and refining results. Available as a Microsoft Copilot or ChatGPT edition.

05

Teams sharing a workflow

Rethinking Work with AI

Delays, workarounds, and difficult handoffs are often signs that a workflow needs attention before new technology is added. This collaborative working session brings together people who share a process to surface friction, understand dependencies, and explore improvements across roles, communication, steps, and tools. Participants examine possible changes together and identify ideas worth testing. AI may help challenge assumptions or investigate options, but it is not assumed to be the solution.

Shaped around your needs.

These are adaptable engagement concepts, not fixed, off-the-shelf courses. Participants, scope, preparation, and virtual delivery are agreed around the organization's situation. The goal is to move beyond awareness toward useful practice, clearer choices, or a practical next step.
